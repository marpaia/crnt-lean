## 2. Mathematical notes and statement-fidelity remarks

* **A5** is stated for indexed reactions; parallel reactions and self-loops are linearly
  dependent and correctly destroy identifiability (non-vacuity example in
  `test/NewLeavesAudit.lean`).
* **S18 / Joshi–Shiu.** The naive converse "multistationary ⇒ alternating arrow pattern" is
  *false*: `A → 2A`, `A → 0` with equal rates makes every positive point a steady state but has
  only one reactant coefficient. The proved characterization assumes the steady-state polynomial
  is not identically zero. The polynomial step is an elementary one-sign-change Descartes
  argument (`Polynomial.no_two_pos_roots_of_no_alternation`) rather than Mathlib's
  `signVariations`.
* **F10/S16.** Necessity of the sign condition constructs rate constants from a positive
  circulation (`exists_rateConstants_complexBalanced_at`); sufficiency was already in the library.
* **M11** is proved only for full stoichiometric rank (fully open networks); `hS` is a hypothesis
  although it is implied by nondegeneracy.
* **P6** survival probabilities are the finite-sum recursion of the killed jump chain; they are
  not identified with a path measure.
* **P7** proves the deterministic criterion and the almost-sure divergence of jump times; the
  continuous-time chain itself is not constructed, and `P(E ≥ 1) = e⁻¹` for unit exponentials is
  not computed (hypotheses shown satisfiable with i.i.d. `δ₁`).
* **P10** jump matrix is identified with the library kernel (`jumpMatrix_eq_jumpKernel`).
* **C3** stochastic setpoints assume the first-moment stationarity identity.
* **S17** assumes the binomial description; Theorem 3.8's criterion is proved as a sufficient
  condition with `A ⇌ B` as instance.
* **D19** contraction is at frozen substrate; it is not wired into the coupled QSSA theorems.
* **E3** has no existence theory; only constant solutions and conserved functionals.
