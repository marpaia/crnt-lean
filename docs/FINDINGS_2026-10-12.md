## Main theorems

* **Strongly endotactic networks are permanent** (Gopalkrishnan–Miller–Shiu 2014, Theorem 1.1), for
  fixed and for tempered rates `ε ≤ κ_r(t) ≤ ε⁻¹`: one compact set per positive class absorbs every
  solution from the class (`StronglyEndotacticStd.permanent_varK`, `permanent_trajectories`).
  Ingredients: the uniform (variable-rate) tier negativity, proved from the fixed-rate form by
  domination (`TierDescending.uniformTierDissipationEventuallyNegative`, a former named input);
  persistence by induction on the set of species that may become small, using species projection
  and the projected tier entropy (`persistent_step`, `persistent_uniform`), which replaces the
  boundary argument of Anderson–Cappelletti–Kim–Nguyen Lemma 5.3; entry into a fixed compact set
  and assembly (ACKN Theorem 5.5).
* Consequences: GAC for complex-balanced strongly endotactic systems (GMS Theorem 1.2,
  `StronglyEndotacticStd.gac`); weakly reversible networks whose linkage classes span the
  stoichiometric subspace are strongly endotactic and permanent (GMS Corollaries 3.19, 8.9), with a
  two-linkage-class instance; weakly reversible networks of stoichiometric rank one are permanent
  (`permanent_varK_of_stoichRank_one`); unconditional single-linkage GAC.
* **First-order endotactic networks are persistent** (`IsFirstOrder.persistent`).
* Joshi–Shiu 2017 two-reaction classification (Theorems 5.1, 5.2, 5.8, 5.12) and
  Bihan–Dickenstein–Giaroli lower bounds (decorated simplices, with conservation laws), with
  instances; openness of nondegenerate multistationarity regions.
* Gröbner bases, elimination, Buchberger's criterion (A2); lifting of nondegenerate tropical
  equilibrations (A4); Volterra's law of averages (D18).
* Monotone systems: structural and signed-graph (Harary) criteria for orthant monotonicity;
  Euclidean contraction (D14).
* Continuous-time chain (P7): Feller's minimal transition function for jump systems, its integral
  equation, continuity and backward Kolmogorov equation on the whole count lattice; stochasticity
  under bounded reachable exit rates, in particular for conservative networks.

## Defects found (each with a Lean proof)

1. **`Endotactic` is weaker than the standard notion.** The library's definition compares with
   sources that are `w`-maximal among all sources; the standard one (CNP, GMS) uses `w`-essential
   reactions. `0 ⇌ X₀`, `X₁ → 0` satisfies the library's notion, not the standard one, and
   `x₁ → 0` (`Examples/WeakEndotactic`). Two frontier claims stated with it were false
   (`TwoSpeciesEndotacticPermanenceClaim`, `FirstOrderEndotacticPersistenceClaim`); both are now
   stated with `EndotacticStd`, and the first-order one is proved.
2. **Flow-based properties are vacuous without a global flow.** `IsMassActionFlow` requires a flow
   on all of `ℝ^S`; for `2X → 0` none exists (blow-up from `x(0) = −1`), so
   `StructurallyPersistentStd`, `StructurallyPermanent` and `HasGlobalAttractorProperty` hold
   vacuously while positive solutions tend to `0` (`Examples/FlowVacuity`). This affects
   `PersistenceConjecture` and the flow-form statements of D5 and D9; trajectory-form statements
   (`PersistentFrom`, `GenuinePermanentForRates`, `complexBalanced_trajectory_converges`) are pinned
   alongside.
3. **`NoDrainableSiphonUniformPersistenceClaim` is false.** `X → 2X`, `X + Y → X`, `0 → Y` has no
   drainable siphon and an explicit global flow, but `y` is not bounded below
   (`Examples/NoDrainableUnbounded`). The Deshpande–Gopalkrishnan theorem is about bounded orbits.
4. Citation numbering for GMS 2014 corrected in docstrings (Thm 1.1 permanence, 1.2 GAC, 1.3 one
   linkage class, 8.5 persistence).
